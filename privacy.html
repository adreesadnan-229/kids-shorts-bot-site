Privacy Policy – Kids Shorts Bot

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Kids Shorts Bot is a personal automation tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data we access

The app requests only the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) for the owner's Google account. It uses this permission solely to upload videos that the tool creates.

Data we store or share

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of viewers or other users. The owner's authorization token is stored privately as an encrypted secret and is used only to upload videos.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
